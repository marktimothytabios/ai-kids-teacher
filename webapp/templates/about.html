About Little Whys

Toddlers ask “why?” all day long. We wanted a gentle, screen-free way to answer, and to share the values we hope they grow up with.

Each book has 20 short stories on one theme. Together they cover knowledge, kindness and empathy, cleanliness and healthy habits, safety and practical maths. There is no app, no account and no advertising.

How the books are made

Every story is written once and built into both US and UK spelling. A checker makes sure every story has a question, an answer, an activity and something to talk about. The pictures are drawn by code for now, and we plan to commission an illustrator as the series grows.

Free for now

While we launch, every book is free to download. If you enjoy them, tell another parent.
